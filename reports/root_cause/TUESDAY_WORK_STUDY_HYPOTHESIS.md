# Tuesday work/study hypothesis and nearby-business investigation

The proposed explanation is a hypothesis: flexible working schedules, travel conditions, or a preference to work/study in a café might contribute to Tuesday demand. The transaction data do not identify work schedules, customer occupations, travel conditions, countries, visit purpose, nearby establishments or time spent seated. Do not describe work-from-home days or lower Tuesday traffic as verified facts.

## What the existing transactions show

Original source: January–June 2026. Aggregate recorded lines within unique transaction ID before comparing visit characteristics. Compare Tuesday with Monday/Wednesday/Thursday/Friday; exclude weekends from this comparison. These are unadjusted descriptive shares, not causal estimates or formal tests.

Counts above are totals over unequal numbers of dates, not daily demand comparisons. The previously established Tuesday daily-demand premium is documented separately in REPORT.md.

Tuesday does not show an obvious network-wide increase in the *share* of dine-in or daytime transactions. Garden's dine-in share is 59.45% Tuesday versus 57.93% other weekdays; Harbour's is 56.68% versus 59.54%; Uptown's is 60.64% versus 60.27%. The direction is not uniform across branches.

These observations do not establish the work/study explanation. They also do not rule it out: more total Tuesday traffic could include more workers while leaving proportions similar, and dine-in/daytime are weak proxies for purpose. Visit duration, customer intent, verified local schedules and comparison conditions are needed. The same caution applies to recurring events, promotions and data-provenance explanations.

## Location information required

The CSV contains only the branch labels Garden, Harbour and Uptown, with no addresses, coordinates, city or country. Do not infer actual locations from those labels, the brand name or the user's reference to Lebanon. Verified addresses/Maps links and confirmation of whether the labels are anonymized are required before searching nearby places.

Once locations are supplied, create an auditable nearby-place inventory using public sources: offices/business parks, universities, schools, transit stops, coworking spaces and competing cafés. Record source URLs, retrieval dates and distance/walking-time assumptions. Establishments are contextual indicators, not proof of customer occupations or individual demographics. Use aggregate context rather than identifying or profiling individual customers.

## Tests that distinguish explanations

| Hypothesis | Evidence to obtain | Discriminating comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Customers work/study in the café on Tuesdays | Voluntary short visit-purpose survey; dwell/seat occupancy observations; verified local schedules | Purpose and occupancy by branch, weekday and hour; include similar non-Tuesday periods |
| Less traffic improves accessibility | Verified local traffic/transport data for the actual branch dates and routes | Date/hour differences alongside promotions, holidays and local events; association alone remains noncausal |
| A recurring offer or event attracts Tuesday visitors | Complete campaign/event calendar, including channels outside POS | Exposure/eligibility and net GP, not simply discounted versus full-price completed purchases |
| A shared calendar or data-construction rule produces the pattern | Data provenance and documented transformations | Confirm whether dates or demand were altered before collection; avoid operational recommendations from artifacts |

## Financially testable actions

If verified nearby employers and a customer survey support an employee audience, consider a small employer-partnership or pre-order trial. If work/study visits are supported, consider a limited off-peak work/study proposition. These are alternative experiments, not demonstrated profitable recommendations. Do not target a discount merely because Tuesday is already busy.

Predefine eligibility and assignment, keep a comparable control, and measure total GP per eligible opportunity after marketing/service costs. For seated work/study visits, also measure seat occupancy and dwell time: a higher transaction count need not improve GP per occupied seat-hour. Track displacement of other customers and purchases. No extra sales, wage effect, traffic effect or financial upside is quantified by the current file.

A machine-learning location-response model is not warranted from three branch labels without verified location features and observed campaign responses. Begin with the geographic evidence and a controlled test. Existing forecasts can support timing; they do not identify the cause of Tuesday demand or predict the effect of employer partnerships.
